CanyonGlow icon
---------------
The current picture/CanyonGlow.ico is original geometric artwork created for
CanyonGlow: canyon walls and three coloured light paths. It uses no product
photographs, manufacturer logos or external artwork. Source artwork and the
generator are available as picture/CanyonGlow.svg and make_icon.py in the
source repository. The PNG, SVG, ICO and generator use CanyonGlow's MIT
license.
